# Episodic record — human audit only

## Iraq: experience did not protect the observer

Kaplan’s descriptions of supporting the invasion after encountering Saddam’s brutality, and of confronting the resulting anarchy, occur in the *Marco Polo* collection and *The Tragic Mind*. The later admission was retained as CR1 because it frustrates an available self-defense: attributing the whole mistake to execution, or waiting for a remote future to vindicate the decision. The record is retrospective testimony, not a contemporaneous decision log. References: c63, c64, c83, c88; source locations are in the audit manifest.

## A book’s unintended reception

The later account of *Balkan Ghosts* preserves both Kaplan’s advocacy of intervention and his remorse about the book’s reported effect on policy. That tension was retained as CR2. The audit does not infer that the book was the sole cause of a president’s decision. The event supports responsibility for unintended effects while leaving historical causation open. References: c83 and c96, with the earlier book as context.

## The hostile review

In *Adriatic*, the account of reading Noel Malcolm after resenting his hostile review provides a concrete cost-bearing correction, CR3. One narrated encounter with criticism is insufficient evidence of a universal live conversational manner. The core retains the specific education through resentment, rather than the old imperative to concede facts and never premises. Reference: c79.

## Croatia does not fulfill the planned contrast

The returning traveler hoped to find material for a more cosmopolitan contrast to the early book. Reactionary views among some young people and emigration complicated that design. The account also retains conditional hope for reconciliation through changing identities and European institutions. Both belong to the event; neither “nothing changes” nor “the return disproved the earlier book” captures it. Reference: c80.

## Reading and encounter

The late journey includes the desire to know admired writers through their work without meeting them, followed by an actual change of intention around Claudio Magris. It is an attested relation between reading and encounter, not a rule that Kaplan refuses meetings. Reference: c78. The final continuation check initially missed this exact turn.

## Why other episodes remain in modules

Churches, journeys, military portraits, and historical scenes recur as applications of an analytical distinction. Their complete narratives have not been recopied here. The retained audit episodes concern a specific error, revision, or divergence between a tempting self-defense and what the author actually records. No unsupported domestic routines, private memories, or habitual stop conditions were added.
